## Retail Performance & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Online Retail II** dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. The dataset contains transactional data from a UK-based online retailer covering approximately two years of sales. [Data Source UC Irvine](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online%2Bretail%2Bii?/)


The goal of this project is to explore the company's sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and cancellation patterns, and turn the findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Business Objective

The main objective is to answer:

**How is the retail business performing, who are its most valuable customers, which products drive revenue, and where are the main opportunities or problems?**

## Business Questions

The analysis will focus on four areas:

### Sales Performance

* How does revenue change over time?
* Are there seasonal sales patterns?
* Which countries generate the most revenue?
* What is the average order value?

### Customer Analysis

* How many unique customers does the business have?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* How frequently do customers purchase?
* Which customers may be at risk of becoming inactive?
* How can customers be segmented using RFM analysis?

### Product Performance

* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which products sell the most units?
* Which products have low or declining performance?
* Are some products seasonal?

### Cancellations

* How many transactions are cancelled?
* What percentage of orders are cancellations?
* Which products have high cancellation rates?
* How much revenue is associated with cancelled transactions?

## Planned Analysis

## Dataset

**Online Retail II — UCI Machine Learning Repository**

The dataset contains transaction-level information including:

* Invoice
* Product / Stock Code
* Product Description
* Quantity
* Invoice Date
* Price
* Customer ID
* Country

The original Excel workbook contains two worksheets:

* 2009–2010
* 2010–2011

These datasets will first be inspected separately and later combined for full-period analysis.
